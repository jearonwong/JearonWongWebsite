# AIIRWP Post-Release Monitoring SOP

**Status:** prepared for owner execution. No monitoring execution is claimed in R17.

## Day 0 Checks

- Confirm production route loads.
- Confirm HTML/PDF/manifest/checksum links load.
- Confirm no public DOCX appears.
- Confirm social/newsletter links use correct route and PDF URLs.
- Capture owner execution URLs only after publication.
- Watch for immediate misinterpretation risk in comments or replies.

## Day 1 Checks

- Recheck production route and artifact links.
- Review comments and replies for misunderstanding of insurance/legal boundaries.
- Record any inbound emails or messages.
- Capture screenshots of executed posts if owner has published.
- Check search manually only as observation, not as indexing proof unless evidence exists.

## Day 3 Checks

- Check backlinks and mentions.
- Check search visibility across selected queries.
- Check AI answer visibility across selected engines only if owner chooses to test.
- Record any source-specific evidence in the authority and evidence trackers.

## Day 7 Checks

- Recheck links, metadata, and PDF availability.
- Review engagement patterns and substantive questions.
- Record any credible citations or reposts.
- Document any boundary-risk comments that require clarification.

## Day 14 Checks

- Repeat search and AI answer checks if owner is monitoring discoverability.
- Record snippets, answer citations, or absence of evidence without overclaiming.
- Review whether follow-up explanatory content is needed.

## Day 30 Checks

- Summarize evidence collected.
- Separate observed evidence from interpretation.
- Identify follow-up content opportunities.
- Decide whether a later Final Seal request is appropriate; do not infer it from monitoring.

## Rules

- No indexing claim without evidence.
- No adoption claim without evidence.
- No endorsement claim without explicit source language.
- No insurer acceptance claim.
- No legal advice claim.
- No insurance advice claim.
- No coverage opinion claim.
- No underwriting standard claim.
- No claims approval guidance claim.
