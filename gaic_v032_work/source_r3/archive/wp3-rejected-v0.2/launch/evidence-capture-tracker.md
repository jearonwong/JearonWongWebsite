## Evidence Rules

- No fabricated evidence.
- No adoption claim without source-specific evidence.
- No indexing claim without dated search evidence.
- No answer-engine recognition claim without captured answer and citation evidence.
- No endorsement claim without explicit source language.
- No insurer acceptance claim.
- No legal or insurance advice claim.
