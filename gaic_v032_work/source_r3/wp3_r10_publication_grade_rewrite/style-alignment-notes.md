# WP3-R10 Style Alignment Notes

## Target Style

The body should feel like GAIC and AIAAWP: direct, careful, source-aware, and serious enough for an insurer, broker, reinsurer, CRO, CCO, CFO, or counsel to read without fighting the prose.

## Keep

- Plain-English first.
- Strong thesis early.
- Reader pain before vocabulary.
- Tables only where they clarify.
- Clear boundary language in every chapter.
- Analytical terms introduced late and defined plainly.
- Tight, disciplined sentences.

## Avoid

- Generic legal memo tone.
- Generic academic serif-report tone.
- Checklist-heavy consulting tone.
- Source-pack language in the main body.
- Excessive internal shorthand.
- Overclaiming through tone.
- Public-release phrasing in source-only material.

## Visual System Note

The later publication shell should reuse the restrained GAIC / AIAAWP visual family. R10 itself only defines the body and the boundary for future artifact generation.
