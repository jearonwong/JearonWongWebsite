# WP3-R10 Source-Note Conversion Plan

## Goal

Convert the earlier raw source-tag habit into publication-friendly source notes without turning the body into a register dump.

## Conversion Rules

1. Use numbered source notes in the body, not raw source tags.
2. Keep one source note per cluster of related claims.
3. Let the body stay readable without exposing the full internal source inventory.
4. Put detailed source IDs in Appendix A and the supporting R0-R9 reports.
5. Mark authored synthesis explicitly as "this paper defines" or equivalent when first introduced.
6. Keep AIO and AIRM clearly identified as analytical vocabulary.

## Body Usage Rule

- Use [1] when the sentence needs insurance subject or policy-structure grounding.
- Use [2] when the sentence needs market-edge AI insurance grounding.
- Use [3] when the sentence needs claims or incident-response grounding.
- Use [4] when the sentence needs technical context for tools, agents, or protocols.
- Use [5] and [6] when the sentence needs source-truth translation from WP1 or WP2.
- Use [7] when the sentence is WP3 synthesis.
- Use [8] when the sentence mentions dependency, concentration, or accumulation.
- Use [9] when the sentence references AI governance or regulatory pressure.

## Do Not

- Do not paste raw marker strings into the body.
- Do not turn source notes into a full source inventory.
- Do not move QA records into the narrative chapters.
