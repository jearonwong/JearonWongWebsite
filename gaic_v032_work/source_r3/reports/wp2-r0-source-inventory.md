## Candidate Source Use Rules

- Use Big Four sources as market and reader-context sources only.
- Use PCAOB, IAASB, AICPA, ISACA, and IIA for audit, assurance, evidence, controls, and professional-language support.
- Use NIST, OECD, ISO, EU, Singapore, and ICO sources for AI governance, risk, monitoring, human oversight, accountability, and privacy context.
- Use W3C PROV, OpenTelemetry, NIST log guidance, and audit evidence sources together when arguing that logs are useful but incomplete without responsibility-linked evidence chains.
- Use ISO CASCO, ISO/IEC 17029, IAASB, and AICPA sources to protect the boundary between non-certifying evidence validation and certification, conformity assessment, attestation, or assurance opinion.
