# WP2-R0 Source Scope and Search Plan

**Task ID:** WP2-R0-BIG-FOUR-AUDIT-ASSURANCE-SOURCE-RESEARCH  
**Date:** May 17, 2026  
**Status:** Source research scope baseline  
**Boundary:** This file defines source buckets and search strategy only. It does not draft WP2 body text or assert that any source endorses WP2, GAIC, MPLP, AARM, or Jearon Wong.

## Research Objective

WP2-R0 prepares a source-grounded foundation for **Agentic AI Auditability & Assurance White Paper 2026**. The goal is to identify credible external sources that can support future WP2-R1 outline work and WP2-R2 drafting around audit evidence, assurance language, controls, governance, logs versus evidence chains, privacy-preserving evidence, and third-party validation boundaries.

## Source Priority

1. Official sources from Big Four / global accounting and advisory firms.
2. Official audit, assurance, accounting, governance, and internal audit bodies.
3. Official regulator or public-sector guidance.
4. Recognized standards bodies and professional bodies.
5. Peer-reviewed or credible research papers only where useful.
6. High-quality industry reports only as market context, not authority.

Thin SEO blog posts, unsourced AI summaries, and unverifiable vendor claims are excluded where official sources exist.
