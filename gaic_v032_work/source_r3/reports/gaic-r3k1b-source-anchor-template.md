# GAIC-R3K-1B Source Anchor Template

**Date:** 2026-05-14

## Template Rules

R3K-1B uses concise, visible source-trace sections. Each section is route-keyed so the copy remains page-specific and does not become repeated boilerplate.

Every P1 source trace includes:

- source support level: `direct`, `derived`, or `adjacent`
- source-trace summary
- MRO / RCCS-M / ALCS relation where applicable
- lifecycle responsibility example
- boundary note
- white paper anchor links where R3K-0 identified anchors
- canonical source/concept/governance links

## Concept Pages

Template:

- heading: `White paper source trace`
- source support level
- relation to GAIC chapter/MRO/RCCS-M/ALCS where available
- concise lifecycle example
- boundary: not legal advice, certification, legal compliance proof, regulator approval, vendor ranking, procurement guidance, or MPLP requirement
- links: GAIC white paper hub, Agentic Lifecycle Governance, Concepts Map, plus route-specific HTML anchors

Adjacent concept routes explicitly state that R3K-0 did not assign a direct chapter/table/MRO anchor and that the page is adjacent or derived rather than direct.

## Governance Pages

Template:

- source trace to regulatory baseline, engineering-object translation, MRO, RCCS-M, ALCS, and boundary discipline
- governance mapping relation: regulatory or enterprise language mapped to lifecycle responsibility objects
- lifecycle example focused on authority, evidence, accepted outcome, dispute, remediation, and closure
- boundary: source-qualified, author-analytical, not legal advice or certification

## Systems Mapping Pages

Template:

- source trace to Chapter 12 system mappings, provisional results, scoring method, and boundary discipline
- relation: GAIC-cited system, not vendor ranking or procurement guidance
- lifecycle example: authority, evidence, accepted outcome, substitution, dispute, remediation, closure
- boundary: source-qualified, provisional, non-ranking, not final maturity judgment

MPLP receives the additional Chapter 13 MPLP boundary anchor.

## Extended Ecosystem Mapping Pages

Template:

- source support level: adjacent
- relation: non-GAIC-scored extended ecosystem mapping
- source anchors: substitution MRO, MRO chapter, ALCS, boundary discipline; Cursor additionally receives evidence-validation and closure anchors
- boundary: not vendor documentation, vendor affiliation, product scoring, legal advice, certification, legal compliance proof, or procurement guidance

## Research / Index Surfaces

The extended ecosystem index receives the same adjacent source-trace treatment as the family hub. It remains a navigation surface, not a GAIC scored layer.

## Deferred Families

P2 long-tail essays and playbooks remain deferred. This wave did not convert essays into glossary pages or playbooks into compliance advice.
