# WP3-R1 Source Risk and Citation Boundary Register

**Task ID:** WP3-R1-INSURABILITY-SOURCE-RESEARCH
**Mode:** Source research only

## Source Risk Register

| Risk class | Sources | Citation recommendation | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paywalled or access-limited | CYB-05 may gate full report detail; some broker PDF details may move URLs | Cite accessible landing page and record access note; do not rely on inaccessible detail for core claims. | Access limitation is not evidence. |
| Marketing-heavy product pages | MKT-01, MKT-03 | Use only to prove product/market example exists; pair with broker/association sources for broader market map. | Do not infer market consensus or policy wording. |
| Thought leadership / broker analysis | MKT-05, MKT-06, MKT-07 | Use as market-signal/supporting context; avoid converting into legal, coverage, or underwriting rules. | Broker view is not universal market practice. |
| Regulation/governance but not insurance | AI-01, AI-02 | Use for AI governance and risk vocabulary only. | Do not infer coverage or insurability from AI governance rules. |
| Technical docs | TECH-01 through TECH-05 | Use for technical capability boundaries only. | Do not say frameworks provide claim evidence, coverage authority, or insurer acceptance. |
| Cyber-specific aggregation sources | CYB-01 through CYB-04 | Use as analogy/source support for accumulation logic; clearly label agentic AI extrapolation as author synthesis. | Do not create actuarial model or loss estimate. |
| Internal author framework | INT-01 through INT-07 | Use for WP1/WP2/WP3 continuity and object mapping. | Do not present internal models as external standards. |

## Sources Not to Use for Certain Claims

| Do not use source type for | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Legal conclusions | General insurance glossaries, broker pages, and product pages do not decide legal liability. |
| Underwriting conclusions | Public broker/insurer pages do not establish binding underwriting standards. |
| Broad market consensus | Product announcements and thought leadership are examples/signals only. |
| Claims approval | Incident-response sources and claim evidence maps do not determine claim outcomes. |
| Vendor ranking | Technical docs identify capabilities; they do not support superiority or procurement choice. |
| AIO/AIRM acceptance | No R1 source says insurers accept AIO or AIRM. |

## Forbidden Conversions

| Source says | Do not write | Safe conversion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Some sources discuss exclusions, sublimits, or boundary uncertainty. | "The insurance industry excludes AI." | "Public sources show AI exclusions, sublimits, and policy-boundary questions are active areas of review." |
| A source describes an AI chatbot or AI performance product. | "AI agents are insurable." | "AI-specific product examples exist, but they do not establish broad agentic AI lifecycle risk transfer." |
| Framework docs describe traces, sessions, or persistence. | "Claim evidence exists." | "Technical traces are useful ingredients that require responsibility, authority, loss, causality, and boundary linkage." |
| Professional liability may respond to certain mistakes. | "AI professional liability is covered." | "AI-assisted professional-service losses may raise E&O questions depending on facts and terms." |
| A framework provides tool orchestration. | "The framework provides insurance evidence." | "The framework provides technical records that may be inputs to an evidence chain." |
| Cyber aggregation sources describe systemic risk. | "Agentic AI aggregation is actuarially priced." | "Cyber aggregation offers a source-grounded analogy for concentration and correlated dependency risk." |

## Citation Placement Recommendations

- Chapters 0-3 should use insurance basics plus internal WP1/WP2 mapping.
- Chapter 4 should cite market examples directly and keep caveats in the same paragraph.
- Chapter 5 should cite market/claims/technical sources, then mark the lifecycle object gap as author synthesis.
- Chapters 7, 10, and 13 should cite NIST/CISA incident-response sources and WP2 evidence-chain sources together.
- Chapter 14 should cite cyber aggregation/reinsurance sources, then clearly label agentic AI aggregation mapping as author synthesis.
- Chapter 15 should cite only as support for evidence-readiness concepts; AIRM itself must be labeled author synthesis.
