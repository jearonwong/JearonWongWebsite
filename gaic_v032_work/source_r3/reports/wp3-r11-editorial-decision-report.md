- The R10 body is clearly not a source pack anymore.

A serious publication-grade rewrite skeleton with the right thesis, the right boundary discipline, and the right architecture.

## What It Is Not Yet

A final whitepaper body with enough narrative density and market specificity to go to artifact generation.

## Required Next Wave

R12 should expand the body, especially Chapters 0-6, while preserving the 10-chapter architecture and the body/evidence separation.
