## Core Positioning

WP3 defines the missing lifecycle evidence layer between agentic AI deployment and insurable risk transfer.

## Primary Thesis

Agentic AI cannot become broadly insurable until insurers can distinguish the insured legal subject from the agentic risk object, map human and organizational responsibility to agentic actions, and reconstruct the lifecycle evidence that connects authority, action, loss, remediation, and coverage boundaries.

## Short Thesis

AI agents are not the insured legal subject. They are agentic risk objects whose actions must be mapped back to human roles, corporate responsibility, coverage boundaries, and reconstructable lifecycle evidence.

## Sharp Sentence

Logs do not make AI agents insurable. Reconstructable lifecycle evidence does.

## Terms WP3 Must Define

| Term | R0 definition baseline | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Insured legal subject | The company, person, officer, professional, vendor, or organization that may be named, protected, or legally situated under a policy or risk-transfer arrangement. | Not a legal opinion about who is actually insured in any policy. |
| Insurable agentic risk object | A bounded agentic work unit, workflow, tool-action exposure, delegated operation, or loss-triggering activity that can be described, controlled, evidenced, and reviewed. | Not a policy form or underwriting standard. |
| Human-agent responsibility map | A mapping between human roles, organizational roles, agent/MAS roles, authority, evidence duties, and lifecycle outcomes. | Not a legal liability determination. |
| Coverage boundary | The described boundary between included, excluded, limited, disputed, and outside-scope risk categories. | Not a coverage opinion. |
| Claim evidence chain | Reconstructable evidence linking authority, action, consequence, loss event, remediation, and coverage/exclusion review questions. | Not a claims approval framework. |
| Loss event reconstruction | The post-event reconstruction of what happened, who/what acted, under what authority, through which tools, causing or contributing to what consequence. | Not a causation finding by a court, insurer, or expert. |
| Aggregation / accumulation risk | The view of correlated or concentrated agentic exposures across systems, vendors, models, tools, policies, insureds, or events. | Not an actuarial model. |
| Dispute-ready claim package | A package of scoped evidence that can support review, challenge, remediation, and dispute handling. | Not a guarantee of claim payment. |

## Things WP3 Must Not Define

## Plain-English Reader Promise

WP3 should explain that the company may be insured, while the AI agent usually is not. The agent may cause or contribute to a loss, but the insurer still needs to map that event back to a legal subject, a bounded risk object, a responsibility path, and reconstructable evidence.
