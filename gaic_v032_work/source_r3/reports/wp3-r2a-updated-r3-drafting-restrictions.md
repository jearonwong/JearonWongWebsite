# WP3-R2A Updated R3 Drafting Restrictions

**Task ID:** WP3-R2A-EXTERNAL-SOURCE-ACCURACY-AUDIT
**Mode:** External source audit only

## R3 Must Use

- Approved source-backed claims from the R2A claim support audit.
- Cautious market wording for AI insurance examples.
- Access caveats for automated 403/429 or partial-access sources.
- Replacement/corrected URLs for A2A and LangGraph citations.
- Technical framework wording limited to technical capabilities.
- Insurance/legal boundary wording limited to analytical concepts and evidence needs.
- Synthesis labels for AIO, AIRM, agentic work unit, claim evidence chain, dispute-ready package, and lifecycle object layer.
- Caveats for narrow/conditional AI coverage examples.
- Caveats for cyber accumulation analogy.
- Caveats for jurisdiction-specific sources.

## R3 Must Not Use

- Unsupported broad market claims.
- "The insurance industry believes..."
- "AI agents are uninsurable."
- "AI agents are insurable."
- "Insurers will accept..."
- "Coverage applies..."
- "The claim will be paid..."
- "This is underwriting-ready."
- "This proves legal liability."
- "MPLP makes agentic AI insurable."
- "Validation Lab certifies insurability."
- "Framework X lacks governance" unless source-supported and narrowly framed.
- "Vendor X fails" or any vendor ranking language.

## Required Safe Phrases

- "market signals suggest..."
- "some sources indicate..."
- "in certain policy contexts..."
- "may be treated as..."
- "does not by itself establish..."
- "should be understood as an analytical object, not a standard"
- "author synthesis"
- "not a coverage opinion"
- "not an underwriting standard"
- "not claims approval guidance"
- "technical trace, not claim evidence by itself"
- "tool permission, not coverage authority"
- "necessary layer, not sufficient insurability layer"

## Citation Hygiene Rules

- Do not cite `CYB-05` old URL.
- Do not cite `TECH-03` old `google-a2a.github.io` URL.
- Do not cite `TECH-04` as a combined two-URL string.
- Do not use MKT-03, MKT-05, CYB-01, or TECH-01 without access caveats if cited in source notes.
- Do not use product pages or press releases as sole proof of a central thesis.
