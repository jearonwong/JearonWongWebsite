## Chapter 16 Content Summary

**16.0 Why Companion Paper Boundaries Matter**
- Positions main white paper as mother framework for AI Agent Lifecycle Governance
- Explains why scope boundaries are necessary to prevent overload
- Introduces two companion papers: Evidence & Assurance, and Insurability
- Establishes that main paper defines mother framework, not every downstream methodology

**16.1 What the Main White Paper Covers**
- Lists core framework topics: MROs, RCCS, ALCS, composite scoring, enterprise control crosswalk, system positioning, MPLP boundary, evidence-based validation pattern, failure scenarios, adoption roadmap
- Defines framework-entry evidence concepts vs. detailed audit procedures
- Defines framework-entry insurance concepts vs. detailed underwriting methodology
- Explicit boundary: main paper does not define audit engagement procedures, assurance opinions, insurance policy wording, premium pricing, loss quantification, legal compliance opinions, procurement recommendations, certification procedures, or Validation Lab operational procedures

**16.2 Companion Paper 1 — Agentic AI Evidence & Assurance White Paper 2026**
- Defines intended scope: evidence artifact taxonomy, audit control objectives, control activities, test procedures, design effectiveness, operating effectiveness, evidence sufficiency, evidence integrity, replayability, attestation readiness, privacy-preserving validation patterns, validation readiness, assurance engagement boundaries
- Explicit boundary: will not create legal certification, claim Validation Lab is certification authority, issue audit opinions, replace professional audit judgment, guarantee compliance, replace internal/external audit, or define insurance underwriting
- Validation Lab positioning: non-certifying evidence adjudication example only, not regulator, certification authority, conformity assessment body, or legal compliance certification mechanism

**16.3 Companion Paper 2 — Agentic AI Insurability White Paper 2026**
- Defines intended scope: insurability criteria, risk classification, authority boundary risk, tool-action liability, loss attribution, loss quantification concepts, claim replay evidence, dispute-ready evidence, remediation closure, premium differentiation logic, exclusion triggers, autonomous-action exclusions, moral hazard considerations, coverage boundary analysis
- Explicit boundary: will not define actual insurance policy wording, set premiums, replace underwriting/actuarial/legal/claims judgment, guarantee insurability, issue legal opinions, replace insurance broker/underwriter/claims adjuster judgment, or define claim settlement procedures

**16.5 Non-Claim Discipline**

## Boundary Decisions

### Main White Paper Scope
- Defines AI Agent Lifecycle Governance mother framework
- Introduces MROs, RCCS, ALCS, composite scoring at framework-entry level
- Provides enterprise control crosswalk and procurement scorecard inputs
- Introduces evidence chain, validation readiness, tool-action liability, claim replay as lifecycle governance requirements
- Does NOT define audit engagement procedures, assurance opinions, insurance policy wording, premium pricing, actuarial methodology, legal compliance opinions, procurement recommendations, certification procedures

### Evidence & Assurance Companion Paper Scope
- Defines audit control objectives, evidence sufficiency, test procedures, attestation readiness, privacy-preserving validation patterns
- Translates MROs into testable control objectives with evidence requirements
- Does NOT create legal certification, claim Validation Lab is certification authority, issue audit opinions, replace professional audit judgment, guarantee compliance

### Insurability Companion Paper Scope
- Defines risk classification, loss attribution, claim replay evidence, premium differentiation logic, exclusion triggers
- Analyzes insurability implications of lifecycle conformance
- Does NOT define actual insurance policy wording, set premiums, replace underwriting/actuarial/legal/claims judgment, guarantee insurability

### Non-Claim Discipline
- Main white paper is not legal advice, audit opinion, assurance report, insurance underwriting manual, or certification program
- RCCS/ALCS are analytical frameworks, not legal compliance scores
- MPLP is one example of lifecycle protocol, not required for compliance
- Validation Lab is non-certifying evidence adjudication example, not certification authority
- System mappings are analytical tools, not endorsements or certifications
