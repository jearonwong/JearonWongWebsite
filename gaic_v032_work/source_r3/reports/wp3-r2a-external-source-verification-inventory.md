**Boundary:** This inventory audits source existence, accessibility, authority, scope, and risk. It does not provide legal advice, insurance advice, coverage opinion, underwriting standard, claims approval guidance, or insurer acceptance.

- R1/R2 source assignments were checked against the R1 source inventory, R2 source register, live URL checks, and targeted browser/search verification for blocked or high-risk sources.
- Automated access status reflects current command-line fetch behavior from this environment; browser/search accessibility is separately noted where relevant.
- Internal WP1/WP2 sources `INT-01` through `INT-07` remain internal source truth and are not external source objects.
