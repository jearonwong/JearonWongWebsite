## Citation Rules

- Big Four sources are market context only.
- Professional/audit sources support audit terminology, audit evidence, controls, assurance boundaries, and internal audit language.
- AI governance/public guidance supports governance context, risk management, monitoring, documentation, human oversight, and AI accountability.
- Evidence/provenance/observability sources support the logs/evidence distinction.
- Privacy sources support privacy, evidence minimization, selective disclosure, and retention tension.
- Validation/conformity sources support boundary language.
- GAIC is the source for MROs, RCCS-M/ALCS context, Evidence-Based Validation Pattern, Validation Lab boundary, and existing methodology.

## Quote vs Paraphrase Recommendation

- Default to paraphrase.
- Use short exact quotes only for source definitions if necessary and copyright-safe.
- Avoid long quotations from Big Four reports.
- Do not quote ISO standard clauses beyond public summary language unless licensed text is available.
- Legal/regulatory sources may be cited, but WP2 should avoid legal interpretation.
