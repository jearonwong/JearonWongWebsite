## R2 Wording Rule

When a claim is source-backed, R3 may use the claim in a cautious, qualified sentence.

When a claim is synthesis, R3 must mark it as "WP3 author synthesis" or similar on first use and keep the boundary visible.

When a claim is source-adjacent inference, R3 must keep the inference attached to the sources that motivate it and avoid universal language.
