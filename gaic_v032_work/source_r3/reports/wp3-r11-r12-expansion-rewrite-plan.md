## Source Note Changes

- Keep numbered notes for R12.
- Split broad Chapter 5 and Chapter 7 note families into more granular chapter-level notes.
- Reduce repetitive note density where the prose can carry the point.
- Keep AIO/AIRM explicitly labeled as authored synthesis in natural prose.

## Acceptance Criteria for R12

1. Chapters 0-6 feel like a real white paper, not a compact outline.
2. Pain density in Chapters 0-5 is clearly above 4/5.
3. The body reads as continuous argument with natural rhythm.
4. Market reality in Chapter 5 feels sufficiently grounded and specific.
5. AIO/AIRM remain late-stage analytical concepts, not early object dumps.
6. Source notes feel like publication support, not source-pack residue.
7. The paper still avoids legal / insurance advice, coverage opinion, and certification drift.
