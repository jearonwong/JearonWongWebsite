## WP2 Core Thesis

AI agent auditability cannot be built on logs alone.

An agentic system becomes auditable only when its lifecycle work can be reconstructed through responsibility-linked evidence chains.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Agentic AI auditability is the ability to reconstruct, test, and evidence lifecycle work across authority, responsibility, tools, outcomes, exceptions, privacy constraints, and remediation.

## One-Paragraph Thesis

WP2 should argue that the audit object changes when AI systems act through agents, tools, memory, delegation, and multi-agent handoffs. Logs, traces, and observability signals remain useful, but they do not automatically prove who authorized an action, which human role owned it, what agent or tool produced it, whether the outcome was accepted, what exception occurred, or how remediation closed. WP2 turns GAIC's lifecycle responsibility objects into audit evidence objects and defines a proposed readiness model for reconstructable, responsibility-linked agentic work.

## Executive-Summary Thesis Skeleton

- State the market context: audit, assurance, advisory, and governance audiences are already confronting AI assurance, trusted AI, responsible AI, audit transformation, and agentic AI adoption.
- State the object shift: the relevant review object is not only the model or output; it is lifecycle-responsibility-linked agent work.
- State the logs boundary: logs and traces are evidence ingredients, not complete audit evidence chains.
- State the WP2 contribution: map GAIC MROs into audit evidence objects, evidence requests, lifecycle walkthroughs, partitioning rules, privacy-aware disclosure, validation boundaries, and AARM readiness levels.
- State the boundary: WP2 is not an audit standard, certification, assurance opinion, legal compliance proof, regulator approval, procurement recommendation, vendor ranking, or Big Four endorsement.
