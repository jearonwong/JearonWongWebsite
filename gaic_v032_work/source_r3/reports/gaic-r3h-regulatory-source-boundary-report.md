# GAIC-R3H Regulatory Source Boundary Report

Task ID: GAIC-R3H-REGULATORY-AND-ENTERPRISE-GOVERNANCE-SEMANTIC-LAYER

## Boundary

R3H uses official/legal/standards sources only for regulatory or standards context. The pages use cautious mapping language such as "maps to", "is relevant to", "can be analyzed through", and "should be reviewed by legal/compliance counsel." R3H does not provide legal advice, legal compliance proof, certification guidance, regulator-approved guidance, or procurement recommendations.

## Use Discipline

- EU AI Act appears only as regulatory context for human oversight, transparency, record keeping/logging, monitoring, explanation, contestability, dispute, and remediation mapping.
- GDPR appears only as privacy and personal-data context for evidence chain, minimization, data subject rights, processor chain, and retention boundary mapping.
- NIST AI RMF appears only as voluntary risk-management context, especially Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage.
- ISO/IEC 42001 appears only as AI management system context and not as certification advice.
- Singapore IMDA appears only as comparative governance context where used.
- GAIC remains the author-analytical source for MRO, RCCS-M, ALCS, and lifecycle responsibility language.

## Not Added

- No specific legal compliance conclusion.
- No statement that GAIC, MPLP, or any page satisfies a law or standard.
- No "required by EU AI Act" or "required by GDPR" positive claim.
- No certification, regulator approval, official compliance, audit opinion, or procurement recommendation.

## Decision

R3H official-source boundary passes. Regulatory and standards references are source-qualified and cautious.
