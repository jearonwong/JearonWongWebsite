## Rejection Rules Applied

Rejected or avoided:

- Thin SEO content.
- Unsourced AI blog summaries.
- Vendor promotional claims without technical or governance substance.
- Sources that could not be verified.
- Outdated sources superseded by newer official guidance where current guidance exists.

## Citation Posture

- Main-text authority should come from professional bodies, standards bodies, regulators, or public-sector guidance.
- Big Four sources should be used as market context and reader-context only.
- ISO standards pages should be cited as official summaries unless licensed standard text is separately available.
- Legal/regulatory sources should be used as context, not as legal advice or compliance proof.
- WP2-authored constructs must be explicitly presented as proposed synthesis when no external source defines the same construct.
