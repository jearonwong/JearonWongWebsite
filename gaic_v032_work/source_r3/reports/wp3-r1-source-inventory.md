# WP3-R1 Source Inventory

**Task ID:** WP3-R1-INSURABILITY-SOURCE-RESEARCH
**Mode:** Source research only
**Boundary:** This inventory classifies evidence for later WP3 outline work. It does not give legal advice, insurance advice, coverage opinions, underwriting standards, actuarial guidance, claims approval guidance, vendor rankings, procurement advice, insurer acceptance claims, or certification claims.

## Source Tier Rules Applied

- **Tier 1:** regulators, official insurer/reinsurer/broker publications, professional bodies, official AI governance sources, official technical docs.
- **Tier 2:** reputable legal/industry journalism, academic papers, major consulting reports, insurer/broker thought leadership with clear limits.
- **Tier 3:** vendor announcements, startup marketing, unsourced commentary, social posts. Tier 3 is never used alone for core thesis support.
