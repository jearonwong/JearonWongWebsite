# WP3-R2A Insurance and Legal Boundary Accuracy Audit

**Task ID:** WP3-R2A-EXTERNAL-SOURCE-ACCURACY-AUDIT
**Mode:** External source audit only

## Boundary Audit

| Topic | Source support | Safe wording | Forbidden wording | Risk | R3 instruction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Insured legal subject | INS-04, INS-06, INS-08 | "Insurance analysis starts with an insured party, policy subject, or insured role." | "AI agents are never insured" | Legal overgeneralization | Keep plain-English and jurisdiction-neutral |
| Named/additional insured | INS-04, INS-06 | "Insured status is a policy/legal relationship." | "This party is covered" | Coverage opinion | Use only for subject distinction |
| Liability coverage / E&O | INS-07, MKT-06, MKT-07 | "AI-assisted work may raise professional liability boundary questions." | "Professional liability covers AI mistakes" | Coverage conclusion | Use as exposure discussion |
| D&O / corporate exposure | INS-08, MKT-07, MKT-06 | "AI governance is discussed as a board/corporate risk topic." | "D&O coverage applies to AI governance failures" | Coverage conclusion | Keep as governance exposure |
| Cyber policy context | INS-09, INS-10, MKT-03, MKT-04, CYB-03, CYB-04 | "AI-linked cyber risks appear in insurer, broker, and marketplace materials." | "Cyber policies cover/exclude AI cyber losses" | Policy interpretation | Avoid policy response claims |
| Coverage boundary | INS-05, MKT-03, MKT-05, MKT-08 | "Coverage, limits, exclusions, sublimits, and boundary questions matter for review." | "This action is excluded" | Coverage opinion | Frame as review questions |
| Exclusion triggers | INS-05, MKT-03, MKT-05, MKT-08 | "Some sources discuss exclusion, carve-out, or sublimit issues." | "The industry excludes AI" | Market overclaim | Keep narrow and source-bound |
| Claims evidence | CLAIM-01, CLAIM-02, CLAIM-03 | "Incident, response, remediation, and governance sources support reconstruction vocabulary." | "This package will get a claim paid" | Claims approval guidance | Use dispute-readiness language only |
| Legal causation | CLAIM-01, CLAIM-02, CLAIM-03 | "Causality reconstruction is an evidence task." | "The trace proves legal causation" | Legal liability determination | Distinguish sequence from legal causation |
| Underwriting readiness | INS-01, INS-09, INS-10, AI-01, MKT-01, MKT-02 | "Evidence categories may support underwriting discussion." | "This is underwriting-ready" | Underwriting standard | Keep as analytical readiness |
| Insurer acceptance | All market sources | "Market examples and signals exist." | "Insurers will accept AIO/AIRM" | Acceptance overclaim | Never imply acceptance |

## Mandatory Boundary

WP3 may discuss analytical concepts and evidence needs. WP3 must not provide legal advice, insurance advice, coverage opinions, underwriting standards, claims approval guidance, legal liability determinations, or insurer acceptance claims.
