- Guide 2 should remain compliance operating model planning, not legal advice.
- Guide 2 should not become technical implementation documentation; Guide 1 owns that layer.
- Guide 2 should not provide procurement recommendations, vendor rankings, certification claims, regulator approval claims, or audit opinions.
- Guide 2 body drafting remains future work after WP2 source research and framework stabilization.
