# WP2-R0 Audit / Assurance Language Map

**Task ID:** WP2-R0-BIG-FOUR-AUDIT-ASSURANCE-SOURCE-RESEARCH  
**Date:** May 17, 2026  
**Status:** Candidate terminology map  
**Boundary:** This map translates WP2 concepts into audit and assurance language for future drafting. It does not claim WP2 is an audit standard, assurance engagement, attestation report, certification, legal compliance proof, or regulator-approved framework.

## Language Map

| WP2 / GAIC concept | Audit / assurance translation | Why it matters | Source support | WP2 chapter mapping | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agentic Audit Object | The defined subject matter or object of review for agentic lifecycle work. | Audit stakeholders need to know what is being reconstructed and tested. | AUD-01, AUD-02, AUD-03, AI-01, EVID-01 | 3, 5, 13 | Not a new legal category or professional standard. |
| Audit Evidence Chain | A structured evidence relationship linking source records, responsibility metadata, authority, outcome state, exception state, and closure. | Raw records gain evidentiary value when linked to reviewable context. | AUD-01, AUD-02, EVID-01, EVID-02, EVID-03 | 4, 5, 7, 8 | Not a guarantee of sufficiency for every audit or legal context. |
| Lifecycle-responsibility-linked agent work | The agentic work unit plus human role, agent role, authority, tool action, evidence, accepted outcome, and remediation state. | It shifts the review focus from isolated output to lifecycle work. | AUD-06, AI-01, AI-06, EVID-01 | 3, 5, 8 | Do not imply model governance becomes obsolete. |
| MRO | A lifecycle responsibility object that can be translated into evidence requests and control questions. | Gives compliance and audit readers an object layer. | GAIC Chapter 6, Appendix A, AUD-05, AI-01 | 5, 6, 7 | MROs are not legal mandates or certification criteria. |
| Authority boundary | Delegation scope, conditions, expiry, escalation, and permitted action limits. | Helps reviewers test whether action was within authorized scope. | AI-01, AI-06, AUD-06, EVID-01 | 3, 5, 6, 8 | Do not equate IAM permission with business authority. |
| Delegated authority | A reviewable grant of authority from a human/business process to an agent or tool action. | Agentic systems act through delegation, so auditability needs delegation records. | AI-08, AUD-06, GAIC MRO-02 | 5, 6, 7 | Do not claim legal delegation validity. |
| Confirmation boundary | The point where human confirmation, escalation, or reauthorization is required before further action. | Clarifies when HITL becomes evidence-backed control rather than a generic approval button. | AI-01, AI-06, AUD-04, AUD-06 | 5, 7, 8 | Do not claim human approval alone proves compliance. |
| Human role responsibility | Named human role ownership for intent, review, acceptance, escalation, or remediation. | Supports role mapping and review accountability. | AUD-07, AUD-06, AI-01 | 5, 7, 14 | Do not assign legal liability without legal review. |
| Agent role responsibility | Bounded agent capability and responsibility surface, distinct from human role. | Prevents persona labels from obscuring responsibility. | AI-08, EVID-01, GAIC MRO-03 | 3, 5, 8 | Do not anthropomorphize agent responsibility as human accountability. |
| Tool-action evidence | Evidence of consequential tool use, side effects, authority basis, reversibility, and affected system/data. | Tool calls are where agent output becomes operational consequence. | EVID-02, EVID-03, AUD-05, AI-06 | 5, 7, 8, 9 | Do not claim tool logs automatically prove authority or liability. |
| Accepted outcome | Evidence-backed governance state showing whether an output was accepted, rejected, disputed, or remediated. | Separates output completion from business acceptance. | AUD-01, AUD-02, AUD-06, GAIC MRO-04 | 3, 5, 7, 8, 11 | Acceptance is not legal compliance proof. |
| Evidence request | A structured request for records, mappings, traces, role evidence, authority evidence, outcome evidence, and remediation evidence. | Turns auditability from narrative to reviewable evidence. | AUD-01, AUD-02, AUD-05, AUD-06 | 7, 8, 13 | Not procurement guidance or universal legal requirement. |
| Walkthrough | A step-by-step reconstruction of an agentic workflow from intent through delegation, execution, evidence, outcome, exception, and closure. | Internal audit and assurance teams commonly need walkthrough-style understanding of processes and controls. | AUD-04, AUD-05, AUD-06 | 8, 14, 15 | Illustrative review pattern, not a formal audit procedure. |
| Exception | A deviation, policy breach, failed control, disputed state, or unresolved evidence condition. | Exceptions need ownership, impact, evidence, and follow-up. | AUD-01, AUD-04, AUD-05, EVID-04 | 11, 14 | Do not claim legal violation or audit deficiency without context. |
| Remediation closure | Evidence-backed corrective action and closure state after exception or dispute. | Closure must be reconstructable, not merely narrated. | AUD-04, EVID-04, AI-02, GAIC MRO-16 | 11, 14 | Does not prove legal settlement or regulator acceptance. |
| Selective disclosure | Controlled evidence disclosure using minimization, redaction, pointers, summaries, or scoped access. | Auditability must coexist with privacy and confidentiality. | PRIV-01, PRIV-02, PRIV-03, PRIV-04 | 10, 12 | Not legal advice or proof of privacy compliance. |
| Evidence minimization | Collecting and disclosing only the evidence needed for the review objective. | Reduces privacy and confidentiality risk while preserving reviewability. | PRIV-01, PRIV-03, PRIV-04, PRIV-05 | 10 | Not a universal retention or deletion rule. |
| Third-party validation boundary | Non-certifying review of evidence against predefined criteria or rulesets. | Keeps Validation Lab and similar examples away from certification or assurance claims. | BOUND-01, BOUND-02, BOUND-03, AUD-03 | 12, 13, 15 | No certification, audit opinion, legal proof, or regulator approval. |
| AARM readiness level | Readiness descriptor for reconstructability and reviewability of agentic lifecycle work. | Helps discuss maturity without claiming assurance. | AUD-05, AUD-06, AI-01, GAIC R4B AARM baseline | 13, 14 | Not a standard, maturity certification, audit result, or compliance score. |

## Drafting Guidance

- Use professional audit language for evidence, sufficiency, appropriateness, walkthroughs, controls, and exceptions.
- Use public AI governance language for risk management, monitoring, human oversight, documentation, and accountability.
- Use privacy guidance for minimization, retention, selective disclosure, and privacy-by-design tensions.
- Keep WP2's own terms explicitly proposed and authored: Agentic Audit Object, Audit Evidence Chain, lifecycle-responsibility-linked agent work, and AARM.
