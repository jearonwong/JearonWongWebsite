# WHITEPAPER-R8E-0 Chart / Heatmap / Table Decision Plan

**Date:** 2026-05-19

## Decision Principle

Charts and heatmaps are allowed only when they clarify pattern recognition without distorting methodology, exaggerating precision, or implying ranking, certification, legal compliance proof, procurement recommendation, or final score status.

## Main-Text vs Appendix Pattern

Recommended pattern for dense analytical material:

1. Main text: chart/heatmap/scorecard for pattern recognition.
2. Nearby boundary note: fixed source order, provisional analytical framing, non-ranking.
3. Appendix or follow-up table: full detail for verification and citation.

## Result

GAIC needs the most chart/heatmap redesign, especially score overview and dimension coverage. AIAAWP mostly needs preservation of portrait rubric matrices and better summary-vs-detail sequencing.
