# WP3-R9 Whitepaper Quality Root-Cause Audit

## Finding

The current AIIRWP staged body fails the whitepaper body quality gate. The problem is not primarily PDF layout or artifact integrity. The content object is wrong: it packages internal governance, evidence, source, citation, QA, and object-mapping material as if it were the public white paper body.

## 1. Body Failure

- The body reads like a source register, evidence map, QA checklist, and object mapping package.
- It lacks a sustained reader-facing argument about why Agentic AI creates an insurance and risk-transfer problem now.
- It does not sufficiently explain the industry pain before introducing internal analytical objects.
- It overuses object mappings, source traces, synthesis labels, and matrix logic as primary reading material.
- It does not move an intelligent reader through problem, consequence, argument, framework, and conclusion.

## 2. Publication Style Failure

- The publication tone is too internal and procedural compared with GAIC and AIAAWP.
- The body exposes raw source markers and synthesis markers in forms that belong in an audit/source pack.
- The visual and typographic experience feels report-like rather than publication-grade.
- Tables and diagrams are optimized for completeness instead of reader comprehension.
- Cover/body continuity is weak because the artifact starts from internal evidence architecture rather than a public thesis.

## 3. Structural Failure

- Internal governance materials entered the public body.
- Source register logic, citation hygiene, R2A/R6B-style artifacts, and QA records became reader-facing structure.
- Appendices and source logic overwhelm the thesis instead of supporting it.
- AIO/AIRM appear too early and too densely, before the reader has felt the market problem.
- The relationship to WP1/GAIC and WP2/AIAAWP is over-mapped in the body instead of being integrated as supporting context.

## 4. QA Failure

Previous gates correctly checked:

- artifact existence,
- hashes and manifests,
- JSON-LD parseability,
- route availability,
- viewport behavior,
- PDF page count and page rhythm,
- table rendering,
- source/citation rendering,
- boundary claims,
- no public DOCX.

The missing gate was:

> Is this actually a readable, citable, publication-grade white paper?

This missing gate allowed a technically verified artifact to pass even though the public body did not meet the publication standard.

## Root Cause

The process treated evidence completeness as publication readiness. It allowed the internal evidence/source pack to become the body instead of keeping evidence behind a coherent industry argument.

## Corrective Action

R9 applies the public hold and creates the Whitepaper Body Quality Gate. R10 must rewrite AIIRWP from the thesis outward, with evidence moved into footnotes, source notes, appendices, and an internal evidence package.
