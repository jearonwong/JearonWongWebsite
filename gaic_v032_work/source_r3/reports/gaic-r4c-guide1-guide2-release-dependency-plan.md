## Dependency Rules

- Guide 1 and Guide 2 should not be fully drafted before WP2 framework is source-grounded and stable.
- Guide 1/2 planning baselines can be frozen now because they derive from R4B's WP2 architecture baseline.
- WP2-R0 must source-ground audit, assurance, advisory, internal audit, and enterprise governance framing before public WP2 drafting.
- Guide 1-R0 must remain implementation-neutral and avoid MPLP-only architecture.
- Guide 2-R0 must remain non-legal-advice and avoid compliance proof or regulator approval framing.
- MPLP-specific implementation guides must wait for stable MPLP MRO/evidence/conformance mappings.
- Launch claims require evidence.
