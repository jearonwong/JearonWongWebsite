## What Reviewed Sources Discuss

The reviewed sources discuss several themes relevant to WP2's intended audience:

- AI and generative AI are becoming enterprise governance, risk, assurance, and operating-model issues, not only model-performance issues.
- Big Four and major advisory firms are building market language around responsible AI, trusted AI, AI assurance, AI risk, governance, controls, and audit transformation.
- Agentic AI is appearing in market discussion as a distinct enterprise adoption and governance topic, especially around autonomy, delegated action, operational readiness, and risk.
- Assurance and audit transformation sources emphasize confidence, trust, controls, data, analytics, technology enablement, and governance.

## What Reviewed Sources Do Not Fully Define

Based on the reviewed candidate set, WP2 can cautiously position itself as an object-model layer adjacent to existing audit, assurance, and AI governance discussions. The reviewed sources create market context for the need, but they do not appear to fully define the following WP2-specific constructs:

- An **Agentic Audit Object** based on lifecycle-responsibility-linked agent work.
- **Audit Evidence Chains** that bind execution records to authority, responsibility, accepted outcome, exception state, privacy boundary, and remediation closure.
- A full **MRO-to-audit-evidence mapping** from lifecycle responsibility objects into evidence requests.
- **Accepted outcome evidence** as a separate governance state from output completion.
- Cross-agent, cross-tool, cross-vendor, and cross-runtime responsibility reconstruction as the central audit object for agentic systems.

This gap should be phrased as a WP2 opportunity, not as a criticism of the Big Four or assurance firms.
