## Positioning Statement

WP2 should define what makes agentic AI work auditable. Its job is to make auditability concrete without overclaiming audit authority: lifecycle work becomes auditable when the organization can reconstruct authority, responsibility, tools, outcomes, exceptions, evidence, privacy boundaries, and remediation closure through responsibility-linked evidence chains.

## Brand Governance Fit

WP2 should preserve JearonWong.com's "Architectural Headquarters" posture:
