## Body vs Footnote Guidance

- **Body citations:** primary market-map, subject, claims, and evidence sources should appear in the chapter body.
- **Footnote/endnote citations:** technical docs, market reports, and boundary references that would clutter flow should move to notes.
- **Context only:** vendor/product pages and technical docs used only to show capability should not carry legal or coverage conclusions.
- **Never cite for legal conclusions:** glancing product pages, broker thought leadership, technical docs, or internal synthesis files should not be used to conclude coverage, liability, or insurer acceptance.
