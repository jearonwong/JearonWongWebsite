## RCCS-T

**RCCS-T — Traditional Regulatory Compliance Coverage Score** measures how visibly a system covers traditional regulatory and governance requirements such as risk management, data governance, documentation, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, security, accountability, contestability, and monitoring.

## RCCS-M

**RCCS-M — MRO-Adjusted Regulatory Compliance Coverage Score** measures how well a system can express those regulatory obligations through Missing Regulatory Objects required for agentic lifecycle responsibility compliance.

RCCS-M is this paper's forward-looking regulatory coverage model for agentic and MAS environments. It does not replace current law. It translates regulatory coverage into lifecycle responsibility object coverage.

## Boundary

RCCS-M is an analytical model, not a legal compliance score, certification, regulator-approved benchmark, procurement recommendation, official standard, or legal compliance proof.
