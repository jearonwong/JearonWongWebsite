# WP3-R15B PDF Compression and Readability Audit

## Declaration

PDF_REPAIR_REQUIRED_DUE_TO_COMPRESSION.

The R15 PDF is an internal candidate artifact and should not advance to public staging preparation in its current form. The accepted body source is roughly 9,500 words after candidate-source preparation, and the artifact also includes Appendices A-D, 12 source-note families, multiple tables, and the rendered Chapter 4 figure. Compressing that material into 20 A4 pages makes the PDF feel more like a compact internal artifact than a serious external whitepaper candidate.

## Assessment

| Area | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Page count relative to body | 20 A4 pages is tight for the body plus appendices/source notes/tables/figure. |
| Average density per page | High. Several body pages carry long paragraphs plus table material. |
| Apparent body font size | Generally readable in proof images, but the overall page density leaves little publication breathing room. |
| Line height | Functional but too compact for final candidate comfort. |
| Margins | Usable, but not enough to offset the density created by the 20-page target. |
| Source notes density | Readable but compressed; Appendix A feels like a compact support table rather than a relaxed publication appendix. |
| Table density | No single-character collapse observed, but high-comparison tables are dense and need reproof after layout relaxation. |
| Figure size | The Chapter 4 figure is readable, but it would benefit from a slightly less crowded page context. |
| Appendix readability | Appendix A-D are present and readable, but packed tightly. |
| GAIC/AIAAWP A4 profile alignment | Partial. The artifact borrows the family visual direction but lacks mature print rhythm, page identity, and polished cover fit. |
| External serious reader acceptability | Not yet acceptable. Repair required before public staging preparation. |

## Blocking Issues

- The cover has a visible large white band at the bottom instead of an A4-optimized full-page cover treatment.
- The PDF proof pages do not show a mature header/footer/page-number/document-identity profile.
- The source notes and appendices are readable but too compressed for external publication comfort.
- The 20-page length creates a credibility risk because the artifact does not visually breathe like a whitepaper carrying an 8,500+ word body plus appendices.

## R15C Repair Direction

- Relax print CSS density and allow a higher page count.
- Improve A4 cover sizing to remove the bottom white-band issue.
- Add or restore family-consistent page number/footer/document identity treatment.
- Increase appendix/source-note readability without creating a source-pack feel.
- Re-render proof pages and rerun R15B final candidate artifact QA after repair.
