**Date:** 2026-05-14

- Pending evidence fields.
- To be tested.
- Launch readiness.
- Artifact identity.
- Source-qualified / non-ranking boundary language.

## Result

PASS. R3L creates readiness reports and evidence templates only. It does not claim external launch execution, indexing, answer-engine citation, ranking, backlinks, legal compliance proof, certification, regulator approval, vendor endorsement, procurement guidance, vendor ranking, or MPLP industry-standard status.
