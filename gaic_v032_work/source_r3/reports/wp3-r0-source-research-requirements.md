# WP3-R0 Source Research Requirements

**Task ID:** WP3-R0-INSURABILITY-ARCHITECTURE-BASELINE  
**Mode:** Planning-only  
**Purpose:** Define WP3-R1 source research requirements. R0 does not assert unsourced market facts.

## Research Rule

R0 defines source needs only. WP3-R1 must conduct actual source research before any source-grounded outline or formal draft. No chapter may assert current insurance-market facts, policy behavior, exclusions, sublimits, coverage availability, claim practice, or reinsurance posture until R1 source grounding is complete.
