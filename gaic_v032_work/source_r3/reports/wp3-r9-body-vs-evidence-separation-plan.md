# WP3-R9 Body vs Evidence Separation Plan

## Purpose

Future AIIRWP work must separate the reader-facing white paper body from the internal evidence/source package.

## Whitepaper Body May Contain

- Industry problem definition.
- Real reader pain and practical stakes.
- Plain-English explanation.
- Continuous argumentation.
- Examples and scenarios.
- Selected tables and figures that improve understanding.
- Concise footnotes, endnotes, or source notes.
- A final framework that emerges from the argument.

## Whitepaper Body Must Not Contain

- Source inventory as primary content.
- R2A audit notes.
- Citation hygiene tasks.
- Candidate package metadata.
- Internal QA status.
- Raw source marker tags in the body.
- Raw synthesis labels in the body.
- Build or pipeline language.
- Governance reports.
- Internal artifact status.
- Completeness-driven mapping tables that do not directly support the reader's argument path.

## Evidence Package May Contain

- Source register.
- Citation map.
- Source accuracy audit.
- QA checklist.
- Artifact hashes.
- Boundary sweep.
- Internal mapping detail.
- R2A/R6B/R7B/R8 wave records.
- Source marker preservation records.
- Full source-to-claim traceability.

## Publication Rule

The body must be readable without exposing the scaffolding. Evidence should support the argument through proper citation format and selected appendices, not replace the argument.

## Implementation for R10

1. Treat the current AIIRWP staged artifact as internal evidence/source-pack history.
2. Build a new white paper body from the core thesis and reader problem.
3. Convert raw source markers into footnotes, endnotes, or source notes.
4. Move source inventory, audit records, and QA records out of the public body.
5. Keep only tables and figures that improve reader understanding.
6. Reintroduce AIO/AIRM after the industry problem and risk-transfer gap are established.
7. Require Whitepaper Body Quality Gate approval before any future public artifact generation.
