**Status:** Planning baseline
**Date:** May 17, 2026
**Boundary:** Planning-only. These notes define relationship options and boundaries; they do not create product claims, public pages, implementation guides, or publication assets.

## MPLP

**Relationship:** MPLP is one optional protocol path for lifecycle responsibility semantics.

Guide 1 relevance:

- Can later illustrate how lifecycle work units, authority boundaries, evidence chains, accepted outcomes, and substitution records might be expressed as protocol semantics.
- Must remain optional and not exclusive.

Guide 2 relevance:

- Can later illustrate policy-to-evidence and MRO adoption through protocol-backed objects.
- Must not become the only governance path.

Boundary:

- Not required.
- Not certification.
- Not legal compliance proof.
- Not industry-standard claim.
- Not procurement recommendation.
- Not regulator-approved guidance.

Guide 2 relevance:

## SoloCrew

**Relationship:** SoloCrew is a possible product proof path for human role / AI role / accepted outcome / remediation workflow patterns.

Guide 1 relevance:

- Product proof path for audit-ready workflow primitives if later source-grounded.
- Can illustrate role mapping, work-state transitions, accepted outcome, and remediation workflow patterns.

Guide 2 relevance:

- Product proof path for compliance operating model patterns if later source-grounded.
- Can illustrate human/AI role governance and outcome acceptance governance.

Boundary:

- Do not claim enterprise readiness.
- Do not claim compliance proof.
- Do not claim product certification.
- Do not make SoloCrew a required path.
- Do not use SoloCrew as a substitute for source-grounded Guide 1/2 research.
