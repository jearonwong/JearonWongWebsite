## Appendix Drafting Rules

- Appendices are support structures, not hidden body chapters.
- No appendix should become a standards claim, a checklist mandate, or a certification proxy.
- Appendices should reuse the same source IDs and boundary language as the chapter body.
- Tables in appendices should remain readable in PDF without collapsing comparison semantics.
- Any operational or normative wording must be replaced with evidence-language wording before R3 drafting.
