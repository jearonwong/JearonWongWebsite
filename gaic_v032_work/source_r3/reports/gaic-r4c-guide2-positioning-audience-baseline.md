**Status:** Planning baseline
**Date:** May 17, 2026
**Boundary:** Planning-only. This file defines positioning; it does not draft Guide 2 body text, provide legal advice, create public assets, or claim publication.

## Formal Title

**Chief Compliance Officer Guide to Agentic Lifecycle Governance**

## Subtitle

**An Operating Guide for Turning AI Agent Regulation, Policy, and Audit Evidence into Lifecycle Governance**

## Series Role

Guide 2 is the second practitioner guide in the Agentic Lifecycle Governance Industry Series. It translates WP1's lifecycle responsibility objects and WP2's audit evidence architecture into an enterprise compliance operating model.

## Core Object

**Agentic Lifecycle Governance Operating Model**

## Core Line

AI agent governance cannot remain policy-only. It becomes operational only when policies are mapped to lifecycle responsibility objects, evidence requests, review workflows, and remediation closure.

## Positioning Statement

Guide 2 should help CCOs and enterprise governance teams operationalize agentic lifecycle governance. It turns WP1's MRO layer and WP2's Audit Evidence Object layer into policy-to-evidence mapping, responsibility governance, delegated authority governance, evidence retention, vendor/runtime governance, audit/board/regulator preparation, and incident/dispute/remediation closure.

## Brand Governance Fit

- Architectural Headquarters: Guide 2 defines a governance operating architecture, not generic compliance messaging.
- Evidence First: policy is treated as operational only when it has evidence obligations and review workflows.
- Identity as Infrastructure: the guide extends the authored Agentic Lifecycle Governance system while preserving external legal/audit authority boundaries.
- Restrained Authority: no legal advice, certification, audit opinion, regulator approval, or procurement claim.
- Protocol Architect for the Agent Era: MPLP may remain one optional protocol path, not a required compliance mechanism.
