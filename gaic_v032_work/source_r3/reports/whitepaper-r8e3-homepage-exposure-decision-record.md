# WHITEPAPER-R8E-3 Homepage Exposure Decision Record

## Homepage Role

JearonWong.com remains an entity authority hub and research HQ, not a generic product or blog homepage. The homepage should expose the core research path clearly without becoming a marketing carousel.

## Decision

Replace the single GAIC release module with a restrained Research Series module for the Agentic Lifecycle Governance Industry Series.

## Whitepaper Relationship

| Whitepaper | Homepage role | Copy boundary |
|---|---|---|
| GAIC | Foundation / global compliance | Lifecycle responsibility objects, Missing Regulatory Objects, RCCS-M, ALCS |
| AIAAWP | Specialization / auditability and assurance | Audit evidence chains, AARM, MRO-to-audit-evidence mapping |

## Status Wording

- GAIC: `v0.3.2 Public Edition`
- AIAAWP: `Public research candidate`

This avoids claiming Final Seal, production reverification completion, certification, audit standard status, assurance opinion, legal compliance proof, regulator approval, endorsement, procurement recommendation, or vendor ranking.
