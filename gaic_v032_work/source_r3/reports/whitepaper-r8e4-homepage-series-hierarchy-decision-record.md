# WHITEPAPER-R8E-4 Homepage Series Hierarchy Decision Record

## Current Issue

The homepage Research Series module presented GAIC and AIAAWP as equal-width, side-by-side cards. That treatment flattened the intended relationship:

- GAIC is the foundation / umbrella whitepaper.
- AIAAWP is the auditability and assurance specialization.
- A future third whitepaper should be addable without redesigning a two-card layout.

## Approved Design Principle

The homepage must present the whitepapers as an ordered research series, not as a flat comparison grid.

## Layout Decision

R8E-4 uses a vertical ordered series stack with visible sequence markers:

- `01 / Foundation`
- `02 / Auditability & Assurance`

GAIC remains visually primary through the foundation marker and stronger left rule. AIAAWP remains prominent but reads as a specialization that builds on GAIC.

## Future Third-Paper Readiness

The homepage now renders the series from a data array and uses a vertical stack that does not assume exactly two items. No public third-whitepaper route, CTA, or published status is shown.

## CTA and Supporting Link Hierarchy

Primary whitepaper actions remain:

- `Read paper`
- `Download PDF`

Evidence Registry and Concept Core remain supporting links below the ordered series, not equal to the whitepaper CTAs.

## Boundary Decision

No third whitepaper is represented publicly. No Final Seal, production reverification, certification, audit standard, legal compliance proof, assurance opinion, regulator approval, endorsement, procurement recommendation, vendor ranking, SEO/GEO uplift, indexing, answer-engine recognition, or external adoption claim is introduced.
