# WP3-R11 Narrative Continuity and Rhythm QA

## Assessment

The R10 body has a coherent argument path, but it still feels segmented. It reads more like a disciplined sequence of compact chapters than a fully paced white paper.

## What Flows Well

- The thesis to subject to object progression is clean.
- Chapters 4 through 7 form a sensible bridge from responsibility to claim evidence.
- AIO and AIRM are introduced after the problem is established, which is the right rhythm.
- The conclusion returns to the thesis cleanly.

## Where the Rhythm Breaks

- Chapters 0-3 are clear, but too brief to build enough pressure before the object layer arrives.
- Chapter 5 is the biggest rhythm break because it moves quickly through market reality and then exits.
- Chapter 7 feels a bit like a translation note, not a full chapter.
- Chapter 8 shifts into an object list before the reader has enough narrative runway.
- Chapter 9 is informative, but the matrix arrives almost immediately after the introduction.

## Chapters Needing Expansion

- 0, 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9.

## Chapters Needing Stronger Openings

- 0, 1, 5, 7.

## Chapters Needing Stronger Closings

- 4, 6, 8, 9.

## Tables That Interrupt Rhythm

- Chapter 2 table.
- Chapter 5 market-edge table.
- Chapter 6 evidence-ingredients table.
- Chapter 7 translation table.
- Chapter 8 grouped overview table.
- Chapter 9 readiness matrix.

These tables are defensible, but in their current form they sometimes arrive before the prose has done enough work.

## Overall Rhythm Judgment

The paper sounds serious and credible, but it still has the cadence of a compressed architecture note. R12 should add more connective paragraphs, more concrete examples, and more reflective lead-ins so the paper breathes like a white paper rather than a summary sequence.
