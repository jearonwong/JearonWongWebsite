### Draft Body

The useful sentence is simple: compliance and auditability are necessary ingredients, but they do not by themselves establish insurability. [SRC: INS-01][SRC: CLAIM-01][SYNTHESIS: Jearon Wong]

This chapter should help readers see the lineage. WP1 gives the lifecycle objects. WP2 gives the evidence-chain lens. WP3 gives the insurance translation of both. [INT: INT-01][INT: INT-04][INT: INT-05][INT: INT-06][INT: INT-07]
