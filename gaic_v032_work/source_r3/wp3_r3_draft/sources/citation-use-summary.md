# WP3 R3 Citation Use Summary

## Body Text Rules

- Every major factual claim should carry a source marker.
- External insurance or market claims should use `SRC`.
- Internal framework translations should use `INT`.
- AIO, AIRM, agentic work unit, claim evidence chain, and dispute-ready package should carry `SYNTHESIS: Jearon Wong` on first use.

## Best Source Placement

| Chapter cluster | Preferred sources | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 | INS-04, INS-06, INS-07, INS-08, CLAIM-01 to CLAIM-03, INT-01, INT-04, INT-05 | Plain-English subject/object/responsibility bridge |
| 4 to 5 | MKT-01 to MKT-08, INS-09, INS-10, CYB-01 to CYB-05 | Market edge, fragmentation, and gap framing |
| 6 to 7 | AI-01 to AI-09, CLAIM-01 to CLAIM-03, TECH-01 to TECH-05, INT-04, INT-05 | Compliance to evidence translation |
| 8 to 15 | INT-06, INT-07, CLAIM-01 to CLAIM-03, CYB-02 to CYB-05, TECH-01 to TECH-05 | AIO/AIRM synthesis and evidence architecture |

## Footnote-Only or Context-Only Sources

- Product pages and press releases with access caveats.
- Broker thought leadership with broad market claims.
- Technical docs if the sentence is about insurance, not technical capability.
- Jurisdiction-specific legal pages if the sentence is global or comparative.

## Do Not Cite For

- Legal advice.
- Coverage opinions.
- Underwriting standards.
- Claims approval guidance.
- Vendor ranking or procurement advice.
- Insurer acceptance claims.
