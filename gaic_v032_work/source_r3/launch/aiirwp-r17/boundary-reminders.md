# AIIRWP Boundary Reminders

Use these reminders when editing release copy, replies, outreach, or evidence records.

## Forbidden Claims

- final or sealed status without separate authorization
- certified
- insurer accepted
- coverage-ready
- underwriting-ready
- legal proof
- insurance advice
- legal advice
- coverage opinion
- underwriting standard
- claims approval guidance
- regulator endorsed
- Big Four endorsed
- external adoption without evidence
- indexed or recognized by AI engines without evidence
- public DOCX
- Final Seal
- author release execution without owner-provided evidence
- public announcement execution without owner-provided evidence

## Safe Replacements

- public research candidate
- evidence-backed analysis
- risk-transfer readiness vocabulary
- analytical object model
- lifecycle evidence framework
- source signal
- not final
- not sealed
- not legal advice
- not insurance advice
- not a coverage opinion
- not an underwriting standard
- not claims approval guidance
- owner execution required
- pending owner execution

## Release Copy Discipline

- Say "risk-transfer readiness vocabulary" rather than "underwriting standard."
- Say "analytical object model" rather than "certification."
- Say "public research candidate" rather than "final report."
- Say "evidence capture pending" rather than "adopted" or "recognized."
- Say "source signal" rather than "endorsement."
