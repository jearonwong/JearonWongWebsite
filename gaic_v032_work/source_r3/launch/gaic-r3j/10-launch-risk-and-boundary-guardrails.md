# Launch Risk and Boundary Guardrails

## Prohibited Claims

- No legal advice.
- No legal compliance proof.
- No certification.
- No regulator approval.
- No official compliance claim.
- No vendor ranking.
- No procurement guidance.
- No fake citations.
- No fake backlinks.
- No fake external mentions.
- No search outcome claims before evidence.
- No answer-engine pickup claims before evidence.
- No MPLP industry-standard overclaim.
- No MPLP required/exclusive/certified claim.
- No vendor affiliation or endorsement implication.

## Approved Phrasing

- author-analytical
- source-qualified
- non-ranking
- not procurement guidance
- not legal advice
- not certification
- one protocol path
- lifecycle responsibility compliance
- lifecycle governance mapping
- evidence chain
- accepted outcome
- authority boundary
- Missing Regulatory Objects
- MRO-adjusted coverage

## Risky Phrasing to Avoid

- "GAIC proves compliance"
- "MPLP is the standard"
- "regulator-approved"
- "certified"
- "best vendor"
- "winner"
- "this vendor fails"
- "guaranteed compliance"
- "guaranteed delivery"
- "Google ranks this"
- "ChatGPT cites this"
- "industry has adopted this"

## Evidence Rule

Only claim what has been executed and recorded:

- post URL;
- submitted sitemap evidence;
- Search Console/Bing screenshot or export;
- answer-engine result screenshot and cited URL;
- real external mention or backlink URL.
