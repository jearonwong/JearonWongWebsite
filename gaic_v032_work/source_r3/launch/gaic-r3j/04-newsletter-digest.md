## Longer Digest Version

I have published the Global AI Compliance White Paper 2026 and the surrounding public web edition.

The argument is that AI agent and multi-agent system governance cannot stop at model-level controls.

Model governance remains necessary. But agentic systems create lifecycle questions that model governance alone does not fully answer:

- Who authorized the work?
- What context was active?
- What evidence survived?
- Who accepted the outcome?
- What can be replayed or disputed?
- How does remediation close?

The paper introduces Missing Regulatory Objects, RCCS-M, ALCS, and Agentic Lifecycle Governance as a way to make that missing layer explicit.

The site now includes:

- the white paper hub;
