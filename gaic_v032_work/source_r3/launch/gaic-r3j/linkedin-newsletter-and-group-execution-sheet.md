## Group-Share Copy Variant 3: Enterprise AI / Strategy Audience

Enterprise AI governance is moving from model oversight toward agentic lifecycle accountability.

The question is not only "which model was used?" It is also who authorized the action, what evidence survived, what outcome was accepted, and how substitution, dispute, rollback, and remediation are handled.
