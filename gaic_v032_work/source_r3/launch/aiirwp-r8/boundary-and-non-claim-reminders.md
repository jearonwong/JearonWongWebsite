# AIIRWP Boundary and Non-Claim Reminders

## Non-Claims

Do not claim:

- legal advice
- insurance advice
- coverage opinion
- underwriting standard
- claims approval guidance
- actuarial pricing guidance
- insurer acceptance
- certification
- endorsement
- legal proof
- Final Seal
- external adoption proof
- indexing proof
- SEO/GEO outcome proof
- answer-engine recognition
- coverage-ready status
- underwriting-ready status
- market adoption
- public DOCX

## Concept Boundary

Agentic Insurability Objects and the Agentic Insurability Readiness Model are Jearon Wong synthesis / analytical terms. They are not standards, certifications, insurer requirements, regulatory approvals, policy forms, underwriting rules, actuarial pricing guidance, claims approval frameworks, or legal determinations.

## Safe Replacement Language

| Avoid | Safer replacement |
|---|---|
| AIIRWP makes agentic AI insurable. | AIIRWP frames lifecycle evidence and responsibility mapping for insurability discussion. |
| Insurers accept this model. | AIIRWP provides analytical vocabulary for risk-transfer discussion; no insurer acceptance is claimed. |
| This is coverage-ready. | This may help structure evidence questions; it does not establish coverage-ready status. |
| This is underwriting-ready. | This is not an underwriting standard or underwriting-ready claim. |
| This certifies agentic AI governance. | AIIRWP is research and analytical framing, not certification. |
| This proves legal compliance. | AIIRWP does not provide legal advice or legal proof. |
| Logs are claim evidence. | Logs can be one input to a claim evidence chain, but they are not sufficient by themselves. |
| HITL assigns responsibility. | Human-in-the-loop language must be mapped to role, authority, work unit, decision rights, and evidence. |
| The paper is final sealed. | Final Seal requires a separate owner-authorized wave. |
| The market has adopted AIIRWP. | External evidence must be captured before any visibility or uptake statement is made. |
| AIIRWP is indexed or recognized by answer engines. | Monitoring is pending evidence unless a dated result and screenshot are recorded. |
| Use the DOCX. | Public AIIRWP distribution exposes HTML and PDF only; no public DOCX is provided. |

## Required Public Boundary Note

When posting or summarizing AIIRWP, include a clear boundary statement:

This is research and analytical framing. It is not legal advice, not insurance advice, not a coverage opinion, not an underwriting standard, and not claims approval guidance.
