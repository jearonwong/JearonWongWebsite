# Final Seal Boundary Record

R3AF Final Seal is a repository governance seal only.

It is not:

- Certification.
- Legal advice.
- Insurance advice.
- Underwriting guidance.
- Coverage opinion.
- Regulator approval.
- Insurer endorsement.
- A standard.
- A score or readiness certification.
- Proof of insurability.
- Coverage-ready status.
- Underwriting-ready status.
- Claim-ready status.
- Claim approval guidance.
- Actuarial pricing guidance.
- Premium recommendation.
- Legal liability determination.

## Public Meaning

The public artifact set remains a public research candidate artifact set. The repository governance seal records artifact integrity, route state, and governance acceptance. It does not create an external approval or insurance outcome.
