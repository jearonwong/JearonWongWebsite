# Announcement Boundary Pack

All announcement drafts are `DRAFT - OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED - NOT POSTED`.

## Blocked Claims

Announcement materials must not claim or imply:

- Legal advice.
- Insurance advice.
- Underwriting guidance.
- Coverage opinion.
- Actuarial pricing guidance.
- Premium recommendation.
- Claim approval guidance.
- Legal liability determination.
- Certification.
- Proof of insurability.
- Insurer endorsement.
- Regulator-approved method.
- Procurement recommendation.
- Vendor ranking.
- Score.
- Standard.
- Readiness certification.
- Insurer-adopted method.
- Final, sealed, or release-ready status unless separately owner-authorized later.

## Required Boundary Copy

Use a visible boundary sentence near the end of public-facing drafts:

`This paper is not legal advice, insurance advice, a coverage opinion, underwriting guidance, certification, score, standard, proof of insurability, insurer endorsement, or regulator-approved method.`

## Required Draft Marking

Each draft file must begin with:

`DRAFT - OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED - NOT POSTED`

## Channel Gate

No LinkedIn, X, newsletter, website note, outreach, or social asset execution is authorized in R3AE.
