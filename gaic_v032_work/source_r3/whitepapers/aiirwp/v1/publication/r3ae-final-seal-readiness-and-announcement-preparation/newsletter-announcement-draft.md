## Agentic AI Insurability Requires More Than Auditability

I have prepared the third paper in the Agentic Lifecycle Governance Industry Series:

**Agentic AI Insurability & Risk Transfer White Paper 2026**

The paper asks what has to become reconstructable before agentic AI risk can be discussed responsibly in insurance and enterprise risk-transfer contexts.

Its position is deliberately bounded. Auditability is not insurability. Evidence is not insurance. Governance is not claim approval. A lifecycle evidence chain can support review, reconstruction, and dispute analysis, but it does not itself create coverage, pricing, acceptance, or claim outcome.

The paper examines insured legal subject separation, agentic risk objects, underwriting-facing reviewability, claim evidence chains, and the role of protocolized lifecycle evidence. MPLP appears as one protocol path for evidence and reconstruction, not as certification, a score, a standard, or an insurer-adopted method.
