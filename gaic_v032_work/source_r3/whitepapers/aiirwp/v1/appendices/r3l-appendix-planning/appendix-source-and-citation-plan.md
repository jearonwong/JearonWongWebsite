# Appendix Source and Citation Plan

## Purpose

This plan defines source and citation handling for later appendix drafting. It does not add new sources and does not perform source-strengthening research.

## Source Reference Continuity

The R3J revised internal body preserves source refs `[1]` through `[62]`. R3L plans to preserve that source-reference sequence in later appendix drafting.

Appendices should reuse existing source refs where they summarize body-supported concepts. They should not create new source IDs unless a later source-strengthening wave explicitly adds and registers new sources.

## Source-Note Summaries

Appendix F may later summarize source groups rather than repeating full chapter notes. Any summary must preserve the distinction between:

- market/context sources;
- product examples;
- governance and incident-response sources;
- cyber/cloud/reinsurance analogy sources;
- WP1/WP2 internal analytical foundations;
- author synthesis.

## Author Synthesis Labels

Appendices A and B must preserve author synthesis labels. AIO/AIRM concepts are authored analytical constructs only and not external standards, scores, certifications, insurer-adopted methods, policy conditions, or market labels.

Appendices C, D, and E should label optional evidence structures, reconstruction structures, and boundary maps as analytical structures, not external requirements.

## Rejected v0.2 Exclusion

Rejected v0.2 remains excluded from source truth. It may appear only in governance/history notes as rejected historical context and must not be cited as current body support or appendix support.

## Market and Analogy Caveats

Market and product sources remain context, not proof of market-wide acceptance. Cyber, cloud, reinsurance, governance, and incident-response sources remain analogy/context unless directly tied to AI or agentic AI.

No appendix should imply that cited market, regulatory, broker, insurer, reinsurer, vendor, or framework sources endorse the paper's models.

## Source Gaps

Current source gaps are strengthening opportunities, not blockers before appendix drafting:

- exact exclusion / sublimit primary wording;
- insurer claim documentation;
- AI underwriting questionnaires.

R3L does not recommend source strengthening before R3M appendix drafting. If R3M attempts to add insurer-specific, claims-handling, regulatory, reinsurance, broker, or vendor-specific factual claims beyond the existing body, source strengthening must be separately scoped.

## Citation Style for R3M

R3M should:

- reuse existing source refs when appendix text restates body-supported claims;
- cite body tables and chapters when appendices are reference summaries;
- mark author inference where support is analytical rather than direct source fact;
- keep unresolved source gaps visible;
- avoid unsupported external claims.

## Decision

Source strengthening is not required before appendix drafting. It remains a later optional strengthening wave.
