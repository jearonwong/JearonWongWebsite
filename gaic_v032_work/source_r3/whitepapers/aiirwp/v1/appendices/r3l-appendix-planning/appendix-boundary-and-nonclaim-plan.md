# Appendix Boundary and Non-Claim Plan

## Purpose

This plan prevents appendices from becoming standards, checklists, certifications, insurer-adopted methods, or public-release claims.

## Global Appendix Boundary

All appendices must remain internal reference material until a later wave separately authorizes drafting, QA, artifact planning, and any public decision. Appendix planning does not authorize appendix drafting, body revision, public artifacts, public route changes, publication, public candidate status, final/sealed status, or release-ready status.

## Appendix-Specific Boundaries

### Appendix A - Agentic Insurability Object Model Reference

The object model is not a policy definition, coverage trigger, standard, certification, proof of insurability, insurer endorsement, regulator-approved method, or insurer-adopted method. It is an authored analytical model for organizing risk-object reviewability.

### Appendix B - Non-Scoring Agentic Insurability Reasoning Model

The reasoning model is not a score, grade, benchmark, rating model, readiness certification, underwriting rule, insurer appetite statement, market-acceptance label, or coverage path. It describes reviewability states, not insurance outcomes.

### Appendix C - Underwriting-Facing Evidence Request Structure

The evidence request structure is optional. It is not an underwriting checklist, claim demand, procurement requirement, certification checklist, premium-credit path, binding condition, or proof that any insurer will quote, renew, bind, endorse, accept, price, or pay a claim.

### Appendix D - Claim Reconstruction and Evidence Gap Register

The claim reconstruction register is not claim approval guidance, legal liability proof, settlement guidance, coverage determination, legal causation determination, claim payment instruction, or insurer endorsement.

### Appendix E - Coverage Boundary Question Map

The coverage boundary map is not a coverage opinion, legal advice, policy interpretation, exclusion analysis, sublimit analysis, or claim handling instruction. It only frames questions that remain external to the paper.

### Appendix F - Source and Claim Boundary Notes

Source notes are not legal authority, insurer endorsement, regulator approval, market-wide acceptance, policy-wording proof, or external validation of the paper's authored models.

### Appendix G - Final Non-Claim Language Register

The non-claim register is a publication guardrail and drafting control. It is not legal advice, insurance advice, coverage opinion, underwriting guidance, claim handling guidance, or regulator-approved language.

### Appendix H - Table Inventory and Layout Risk Register

The table inventory is layout planning and structural QA. It is not artifact generation, PDF readiness, HTML readiness, final layout approval, publication readiness, or visual acceptance.

### Appendix I - Assembly and Revision Change Log

The change log is traceability. It is not release certification, artifact integrity proof, checksum validation, public candidate approval, final/sealed status, or publication approval.

## Safer-Language Rules

Use:

- `reference model`;
- `analytical construct`;
- `optional evidence request`;
- `reviewability state`;
- `coverage-boundary question`;
- `source caveat`;
- `layout risk`;
- `traceability note`.

Avoid:

- `standard`;
- `certification`;
- `readiness certification`;
- `score`;
- `rating`;
- `required checklist`;
- `accepted by insurers`;
- `coverage-ready`;
- `underwriting-ready`;
- `claim-ready`;
- `regulator-approved`;
- `public candidate`;
- `final`;
- `sealed`;
- `release-ready`.

## R3M Drafting Constraint

If R3M drafts appendices, every appendix must include concise boundary language and must not weaken the global non-claim discipline established in Front Matter and Chapter 27.
