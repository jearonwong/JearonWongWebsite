## Boundary Result

PASS.

R3E uses "claim reconstruction," "coverage-boundary questions," "remediation closure," and "claims-to-renewal feedback" as bounded analytical concepts. It keeps claim reconstruction separate from claim approval, coverage-boundary analysis separate from coverage opinion, remediation closure separate from settlement/no-liability proof, and feedback loops separate from pricing or renewal outcomes.
