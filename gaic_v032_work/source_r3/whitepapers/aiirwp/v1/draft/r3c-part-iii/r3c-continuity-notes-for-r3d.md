## How R3C Sets Up Part IV

R3C completes the translation layer:

- WP1 MROs become lifecycle governance objects that help describe risk objects.
- WP2 Audit Evidence Chain becomes claim reconstruction logic, not claim approval.
- Insurance line ambiguity is mapped without policy interpretation.
- Aggregation and concentration are framed through shared models, vendors, runtimes, cloud/API dependencies, reusable agents, and evidence systems.
- Privacy, evidence minimization, and selective disclosure are introduced as necessary constraints on underwriting and claim evidence.

Part IV can now move into underwriting-facing architecture without pretending that evidence guarantees coverage, quote, premium, insurability, claim payment, or insurer acceptance.

## R3D Should Pick Up

Recommended Part IV focus:

- underwriting evidence architecture;
- exposure inventory for agentic work;
- premium/exposure variables without pricing guidance;
- dependency and aggregation visibility if further detail is needed;
- renewal/change evidence;
- reviewer-facing evidence requests without creating an underwriting standard.

## R3D Must Preserve

- WP3 is insurability/risk-transfer reasoning, not WP2 auditability.
- WP1/WP2 are analytical foundations, not insurance facts.
- Evidence improves reviewability but does not guarantee coverage or insurability.
- Insurance line ambiguity does not equal coverage opinion.
- Aggregation discussion must not become actuarial pricing guidance or capital modeling.
- Privacy discussion must not become GDPR/legal advice.
- MPLP should remain non-dominant and optional if mentioned at all.
