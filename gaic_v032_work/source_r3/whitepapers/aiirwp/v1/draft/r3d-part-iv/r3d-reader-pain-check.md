## Persona Fit

- Insurers: get reviewability architecture without insurer-adoption claims.
- Reinsurers: get dependency and concentration visibility without pricing/capital model claims.
- Brokers: get a practical evidence and exposure structure without coverage promises.
- CROs/CFOs: get premium-variable language that avoids false pricing precision.
- CTOs/CIOs: get concrete inventory, change, evidence, and tool-action design implications.
- Counsel: gets boundaries around legal, privacy, privilege, coverage, and liability questions.
- Boards: get renewal/change evidence and governance visibility without D&O conclusions.
- AI governance leaders: get a bridge from object/evidence concepts to risk-review architecture.
- Engineering implementation leaders: get practical evidence design categories without MPLP marketing.

## Result

R3D advances beyond R3B/R3C. It does not repeat the Chapter 6 evidence model wholesale, does not turn WP3 into WP2, and does not write premium variables as pricing guidance.
