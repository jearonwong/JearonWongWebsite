- Claim reconstruction is not claim approval.
- Evidence pack is not legal proof.
- Coverage boundary review is not coverage opinion.
- Responsibility mapping is not legal liability determination.
- Remediation closure does not prove no residual liability.
- Privacy/redaction controls remain evidence-design constraints, not legal advice.
