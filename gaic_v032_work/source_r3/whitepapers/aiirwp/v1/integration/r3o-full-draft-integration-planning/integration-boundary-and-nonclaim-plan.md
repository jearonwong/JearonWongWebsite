# Integration Boundary and Non-Claim Plan

## Purpose

This plan defines boundary controls for a future internal integrated draft. R3O does not assemble that draft.

## Core Boundary

The integrated draft must remain an internal draft. Appendices must remain reference material. Integration must not turn body or appendix content into advice, standards, checklists, certifications, insurer-adopted methods, coverage opinions, pricing guidance, claim approval guidance, public candidate status, final/sealed status, release-ready status, or public release.

## Forbidden Positive Claims

R3P must avoid positive claims of:

- legal advice;
- insurance advice;
- underwriting guidance;
- coverage opinion;
- actuarial pricing guidance;
- premium recommendation;
- claim approval guidance;
- legal liability determination;
- certification;
- proof of insurability;
- insurer endorsement;
- regulator-approved method;
- procurement recommendation;
- vendor ranking;
- score;
- standard;
- readiness certification;
- insurer-adopted method;
- public candidate;
- final/sealed;
- release-ready;
- public release.

## Appendix-Specific Controls

| Appendix | Integration control |
|---|---|
| Appendix A | Keep as authored analytical object model only; do not turn into policy definition, coverage trigger, standard, certification, proof of insurability, endorsement, regulator approval, or insurer-adopted method. |
| Appendix B | Keep as non-scoring reasoning states; do not add scores, grades, ratings, benchmarks, readiness levels, certification, underwriting rules, coverage paths, or insurer appetite language. |
| Appendix C | Keep as optional analytical evidence-request structure; do not make it mandatory, checklist-like, claim demand-like, procurement-driven, certification-like, premium-credit path, or binding condition. |
| Appendix D | Keep as reconstruction/evidence-gap structure; do not make it claim approval, liability proof, settlement guidance, coverage determination, legal causation analysis, claim payment support, or insurer endorsement. |
| Appendix E | Keep as question map only; do not answer coverage questions, interpret policies, apply exclusions or sublimits, instruct claim handling, or provide legal advice. |
| Appendix F | Keep as source and claim-boundary notes; do not convert source groups into legal authority, insurer endorsement, regulator approval, market-wide acceptance, policy wording proof, or support beyond scope. |
| Appendix G | Keep as publication guardrail; do not present as legal advice or approved release language. |
| Appendix H | Keep as table inventory and layout-risk plan; do not implement HTML/PDF/CSS layout, claim publication readiness, or create artifact status. |

## Body Cross-Reference Controls

Safe cross-references should:

- say "reference version," "non-scoring reference structure," "optional analytical structure," or "non-claim boundary register";
- avoid "standard," "checklist," "certification," "required evidence," "accepted," "ready," "score," "rating," or "path";
- remain short and navigational;
- not create new claims or change source support.

## Public Status Controls

R3P must preserve:

- WP3 public status: withdrawn / v1.0 rewrite in progress.
- Current public artifact: none.
- Current public PDF: none.
- Public route: noindex under-rewrite status page.
- No public DOCX authorized.

## R3P Boundary Gate

R3P should run boundary QA after integration and before any later QA wave accepts the integrated draft. Artifact generation remains blocked.
