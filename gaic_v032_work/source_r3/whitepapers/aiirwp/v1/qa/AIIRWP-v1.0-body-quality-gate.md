# AIIRWP v1.0 Body Quality Gate

Hard fail if:

- The body is under publication-grade depth.
- The body reads like a mapping dump.
- The body lacks market evidence.
- The body lacks the insured subject / insurable object distinction.
- The body lacks WP1/WP2 closure.
- The body lacks premium variable discussion.
- The body lacks claim reconstruction discussion.
- The body lacks responsibility mapping.
- The body has fewer than 18 substantive chapters.
- The body cannot stand beside AIAAWP visually and substantively.
- The body uses a separate visual identity from GAIC/AIAAWP.
- The public route exposes rejected v0.2 as active.
- The body starts with MPLP rather than insurance-market reality.
- The body treats auditability as automatically equal to insurability.
- The body claims insurance advice, legal advice, coverage opinion, underwriting standard, actuarial pricing guidance, claims approval guidance, insurer acceptance, certification, coverage-ready status, underwriting-ready status, or Final Seal.

## Required Pass Conditions

- Insurance market reality is clear before author models are introduced.
- The insured subject and agentic risk object are separated early.
- WP1 and WP2 are connected as evidence architecture, not as insurance conclusions.
- Underwriting and claims sections are substantive and separate.
- Premium variables are discussed without pricing guidance.
- Source notes are verified and readable.
- Tables and figures clarify decisions rather than inflate the source pack.
- Boundary language is visible but not repetitive.
