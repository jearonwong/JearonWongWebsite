# AIIRWP v1.0 Writing Rules

- Write as a real whitepaper, not a source pack.
- Start from insurance market reality and enterprise pain.
- Use plain English with technical precision.
- Every chapter must answer a real reader question.
- Every table must support decision clarity.
- WP1/WP2 mappings must be explanatory, not dumped.
- No internal QA language in the body.
- No wave IDs in the body.
- No raw source markers.
- No fake citations.
- No unsupported claims.
- No legal advice.
- No insurance advice.
- No coverage opinion.
- No underwriting standard.
- No actuarial pricing guidance.
- No claims approval guidance.
- No insurer acceptance.
- No coverage-ready claim.
- No underwriting-ready claim.
- No certification.
- No Final Seal.
- No external adoption, indexing, SEO/GEO uplift, or answer-engine recognition claim.
- No public DOCX.

## Style Rules

- Make the insurance object problem legible before introducing author models.
- Define insured subject, agentic risk object, responsibility bridge, underwriting evidence, claim reconstruction, and premium variables in the body before appendices.
- Keep MPLP as an optional lifecycle evidence path, not an insurance requirement.
- Treat AIO/AIRM v2 as analytical vocabulary, not product, certification, or score.
- Use market examples only after R1 source verification.
