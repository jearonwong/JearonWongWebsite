# Terminology and Model Framing Audit

## Verdict

PASS.

## Preferred Model Term

`Agentic Insurability Reasoning Model` is the preferred active term in the R3J revised internal draft.

`Agentic Insurability Readiness Model` is not used as active model terminology. `AIO v2` and `AIRM v2` are not used as active model terminology.

## AIO / AIRM Framing

The R3J draft defines the Agentic Insurability Object Model and Agentic Insurability Reasoning Model as analytical vocabulary only. The framing remains authored and internal to the paper's synthesis.

The draft does not turn AIO/AIRM concepts into:

- a score;
- a grade;
- a benchmark;
- a standard;
- a certification;
- readiness certification;
- insurer-adopted method;
- policy condition;
- underwriting shortcut;
- coverage path;
- claim approval method.

## Reviewability Boundary

Reviewability remains distinct from coverage, insurability, claim outcome, insurer acceptance, pricing, underwriting appetite, and legal liability.

The Reasoning Model remains non-scoring. Its states describe reviewability maturity, not insurance outcomes.

## Contextual Hits

Terms such as `score`, `standard`, `certification`, `coverage-ready`, `underwriting-ready`, and `claim-ready` appear only in negative-use or boundary contexts. These hits are safe because the draft explicitly says the models do not create those statuses.

## Result

The model-framing revision is accepted. R3L should preserve the same terminology and should not introduce appendix labels that make the models look like standards, scores, certifications, readiness schemes, or insurer-adopted methods.
