# AIIRWP v1.0 Rewrite Workspace

## Mission

AIIRWP v1.0 must become the publication-grade third whitepaper in the Agentic Lifecycle Governance Industry Series. It must explain how agentic AI becomes legible for risk transfer by connecting insurance market reality, insured legal subjects, agentic risk objects, underwriting variables, claim reconstruction evidence, responsibility mapping, and lifecycle governance records.

## Why v0.2 Was Rejected

The v0.2 public candidate was only 27 pages and did not meet the depth, argument structure, market grounding, underwriting/claims reasoning, WP1/WP2 closure, or publication-quality bar expected for WP3. It is withdrawn and archived for traceability only.

## Relationship to WP1 and WP2

- WP1 / GAIC provides lifecycle governance, Missing Regulatory Objects, authority boundary, accepted outcome, evidence partitioning, substitution conformance, remediation closure, RCCS-M, and ALCS.
- WP2 / AIAAWP provides auditability, Audit Evidence Chain, Agentic Audit Object, and AARM.
- WP3 v1.0 must translate those foundations into insurability and risk-transfer reasoning only after grounding the paper in insurance-market reality.

## Quality Bar

AIIRWP v1.0 must be comparable to AIAAWP or stronger in depth and publication rhythm. It is not a short essay and not a source pack. It must be a publication-grade whitepaper with a serious insurance-market argument.

## Release Boundary

No public release, public artifact generation, production verification, author release execution, or Final Seal may be claimed until owner acceptance occurs in a later wave.
