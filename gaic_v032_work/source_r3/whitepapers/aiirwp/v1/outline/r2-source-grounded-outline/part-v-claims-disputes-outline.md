# Part V Claims and Disputes Outline

## Part Thesis

Claim reconstruction for agentic AI requires more than logs. It needs incident timeline, authority trace, human/agent role trace, causality trace, evidence chain, policy-line boundary, exclusion/sublimit context, and remediation closure. This section must not provide claim approval guidance, legal liability determination, or coverage opinion.

## Chapter 17: Remediation Closure and Post-Loss Evidence

| Field | Outline |
|---|---|
| Chapter thesis | Remediation closure is a post-loss evidence state: it shows what changed, who owned the fix, what was rechecked, and what residual risk remains. |
| Reader pain | After containment, organizations often cannot prove which control changed, whether the agent workflow was reauthorized, or whether substitute components preserved evidence continuity. |
| Opening claim scenario | After an AI payment workflow error, the company disables the workflow, changes authority limits, swaps a model endpoint, and reimburses customers. The post-loss record must connect each action to evidence and owner. |
| Argument beats | Define closure record; map fix/recheck/owner/residual risk; connect substitution conformance; show why remediation matters for future underwriting and dispute readiness. |
| Source basis | CLAIM-NIST-80061R3; CLAIM-CISA-PLAYBOOK; INS-QBE-CYBER; WP1 remediation closure; WP2 closure checklist. |

| Forbidden claims | No claim that remediation proves no residual liability, guarantees coverage, or resolves dispute. |
