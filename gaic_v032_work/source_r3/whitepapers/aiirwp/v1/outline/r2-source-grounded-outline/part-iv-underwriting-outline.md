# Part IV Underwriting Outline

## Part Thesis

Underwriting agentic AI cannot start with a model name. It needs exposure inventory, authority scope, dependency visibility, control/evidence maturity, incident history, remediation records, and aggregation visibility. This section is analytical only and must not become underwriting guidance or pricing advice.

## Chapter 11: The Underwriting Evidence Pack

| Field | Outline |
|---|---|
| Chapter thesis | A credible underwriting discussion for agentic AI would need an evidence pack that identifies the insured subject, agentic work inventory, authority scope, dependencies, controls, incidents, evidence completeness, remediation maturity, and aggregation exposure. |
| Reader pain | Applicants can describe AI strategy, but underwriters need risk objects, controls, dependencies, and evidence that can survive post-loss review. |
| Argument beats | Define evidence pack categories; show why logs are not enough; tie evidence pack to model-performance/cyber/professional liability differences; introduce optional template for Appendix D. |
