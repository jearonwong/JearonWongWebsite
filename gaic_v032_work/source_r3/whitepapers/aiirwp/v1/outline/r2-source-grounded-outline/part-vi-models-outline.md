# Part VI Models Outline

## Part Thesis

AIO v2 and AIRM v2 are analytical vocabularies that organize the evidence and object clarity developed in Parts I-V. They are not standards, certifications, insurer-accepted models, coverage proofs, scores, or pricing tools.
