## Analytical Output

The market is not making one decision about AI. It is making several narrower decisions: some risks are affirmatively covered, some are excluded, some are capped or sublimited, some remain silent in existing lines, and some are addressed through model-performance warranty structures. That split matters because risk transfer cannot be discussed responsibly until the insured subject, covered object, event trigger, evidence boundary, limit structure, and post-loss reconstruction path are separated.

R2 should make this matrix the opening architecture for Part I. The matrix should not imply that all insurers are retreating, that AI is uninsurable, or that affirmative coverage proves broad acceptance of agentic AI.
