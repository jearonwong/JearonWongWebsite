## Claims to Avoid Entirely

- AI is uninsurable.
- AI is broadly insurable.
- Insurers accept agentic AI.
- AIO/AIRM are standards, certifications, underwriting frameworks, or insurer-accepted models.
- MPLP is required for insurability.
- Auditability equals insurability.
- Evidence chain guarantees claim approval.
- Incident response records prove legal causation.
- Premium variables provide pricing or actuarial guidance.
- NAIC/NIST/EU AI governance sources prove insurance coverage.
