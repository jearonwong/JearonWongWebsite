## Freshness Caveats

- Insurance form filings and state adoption maps can change quickly. R2 and any later publication wave must recheck Verisk filing status, NAIC adoption map, and any reported AIG / WR Berkley / Great American exclusion filings before using precise wording.
- AI-linked cyber sublimits are moving quickly. QBE official coverage pages support affirmative AI-linked cyber coverage; FT-derived sublimit reports remain secondary unless direct policy wording is obtained.
- Product pages can change. Archive/citation capture should be performed before any publication wave.
