## Required Analytical Output

Agentic AI insurability needs dependency concentration and aggregation visibility because losses may correlate across shared models, cloud regions, agent frameworks, data connectors, workflow platforms, vendors, APIs, and authentication paths. A single vendor outage, model defect, prompt/tool vulnerability, LLM resource abuse pattern, or cloud dependency can affect many insureds or many business units at once.

The strongest external analogy is cyber accumulation. Cyber insurance has already forced insurers and reinsurers to examine common dependencies and correlated losses. Agentic AI adds a further question: not only "which technology dependency failed?" but "which delegated work objects, authority scopes, accepted outcomes, and responsibility bridges were affected by that shared dependency?"

## R2 Use Rules

- Use cyber accumulation as analogy, not proof.
- Use cloud concentration as dependency visibility evidence.
- Do not provide actuarial pricing conclusions.
- Do not claim reinsurer acceptance of agentic AI.
- Link aggregation to underwriting evidence pack and enterprise exposure inventory.
