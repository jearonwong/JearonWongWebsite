# R1 Research Boundary Notes

## What R1 Is

R1 is a source research package for AIIRWP v1.0. It builds the external insurance-market evidence base and maps WP1/WP2 into bounded insurability reasoning.

## What R1 Is Not

R1 is not:

- a whitepaper body;
- a publication candidate;
- a public artifact package;
- public staging;
- public release;
- a DOCX package;
- a coverage opinion;
- insurance advice;
- legal advice;
- an underwriting standard;
- actuarial pricing guidance;
- claims approval guidance;
- legal liability determination;
- certification;
- endorsement;
- external adoption evidence.

## Source Use Rules

- Primary insurer, regulator, standards, reinsurer, and official product sources can support central claims when the claim fits the source.
- Broker and insurance-market reports can support market framing but not exact policy wording unless they quote and identify primary forms.
- Law-firm commentary can identify issues and source leads but must not be used as legal conclusion.
- Technical framework documents can support governance and evidence context but must not be used as insurance-market proof.
- Internal WP1/WP2 sources can support analytical mapping but not external insurance facts.

## v0.2 Boundary

The withdrawn AIIRWP v0.2 candidate is not used as current body truth, source truth, or drafting base. It may be referenced only as rejected historical context through the registry and rejected archive.

## AIO/AIRM Boundary

AIO and AIRM v2 are analytical vocabularies planned for AIIRWP v1.0. They are not:

- insurance standards;
- certification schemes;
- insurer-accepted models;
- underwriting standards;
- claim approval frameworks;
- coverage-ready proofs;
- MPLP requirements.

## R2 Handoff

R2 should use this package to create a source-grounded outline. R2 should not draft the body. R2 should preserve source-strength distinctions and keep source gaps visible.
