## Required Analytical Output

Affirmative AI coverage examples are real but narrow. The strongest sources describe model-performance warranties, AI product underperformance, AI-linked cyber coverages, or technology liability products. These are not the same as agentic lifecycle risk transfer.

Model-performance coverage asks whether a model or AI product failed to meet a specified performance promise. Agentic lifecycle risk transfer asks whether a loss can be reconstructed across authority, delegated task, human role, agent/tool action, accepted outcome, dependency chain, causality trace, responsibility allocation, and remediation. A product can cover one without covering the other.

## R2 Use Rules

- Lead with product-specific wording: "examples of affirmative AI-related coverage exist."
- Avoid broad wording: "the market accepts agentic AI risk."
- Do not claim any provider accepts MPLP, AIO, AIRM, or WP3 vocabulary.
- Always name the covered object and event type before using the example.
- Place warranty/performance products in Chapter 3 and Appendix A; place AI cyber examples in Chapters 2, 3, 12, and 15.
