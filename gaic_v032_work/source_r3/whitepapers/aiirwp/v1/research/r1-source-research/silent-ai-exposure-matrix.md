# Silent AI Exposure Matrix

Silent AI exposure means AI-linked loss may enter existing insurance lines before a dedicated AI product or exclusion clearly resolves the issue. It does not mean coverage applies.

## Required Analytical Output

"The company is insured" does not solve "which agentic work object caused the loss." A policy may name a company, officer, professional, vendor, or platform as the insured subject, while the loss-generating object may be an AI model, agent workflow, prompt chain, API call, cloud dependency, human approval step, professional deliverable, disclosure decision, or remediation failure.

Silent exposure therefore makes the object problem sharper. A claim file would need more than a policyholder name and a final output. It would need a reconstruction path: who had authority, what the agent did, what tool or vendor was involved, what was accepted, what changed downstream, what evidence survived, and what remediation closed the event.

## R2 Use Rules

- Use silent exposure to motivate the insured subject vs agentic risk object distinction.
- Do not say silent exposure means coverage applies.
- Do not say silent exposure means coverage is denied.
- Keep line-specific caveats visible.
