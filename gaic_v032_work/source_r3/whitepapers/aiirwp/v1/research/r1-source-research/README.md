## R1 Core Finding

The insurance market evidence supports a split-market premise: AI-related risk is being handled through a mix of affirmative AI coverage, warranty/model-performance products, cyber coverage extensions, sublimits, exclusions/endorsements, and silent exposure across existing lines. This does not prove general agentic AI insurability. It does support the v1.0 outline premise that any serious WP3 argument must separate the insured legal subject from the agentic risk object and must define the evidence boundary needed for underwriting discussion and post-loss reconstruction.
