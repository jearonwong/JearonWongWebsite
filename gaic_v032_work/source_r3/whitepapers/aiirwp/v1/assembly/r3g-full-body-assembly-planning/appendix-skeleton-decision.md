# Appendix Skeleton Decision

## Purpose

R3G recommends appendix skeletons for later planning. It does not draft appendices.

## Decision

R3H should not draft full appendices unless separately authorized. If appendix placeholders are needed in the assembled internal draft, they should be skeletons only.

## Recommended Appendix Skeletons

### Appendix A - Agentic Insurability Object Model Reference

Purpose: Provide a consolidated reference for the Agentic Insurability Object Model from Chapter 24.

Boundary: Authored analytical model only; not a standard, certification, policy definition, coverage trigger, proof of insurability, or insurer-adopted method.

### Appendix B - Non-Scoring Insurability Reasoning Model

Purpose: Provide a clean reference for Chapter 25's non-scoring reviewability states.

Boundary: Not a score, benchmark, rating model, grade, readiness certification, underwriting rule, or insurer-adopted method.

### Appendix C - Underwriting-Facing Evidence Request Structure

Purpose: Preserve optional evidence request structure from Chapter 18.

Boundary: Not an underwriting checklist, claim demand, regulator-approved checklist, procurement requirement, certification checklist, or insurer requirement.

### Appendix D - Claim Reconstruction and Evidence Gap Register

Purpose: Consolidate Chapter 19 and Chapter 20 reconstruction/evidence-gap concepts.

Boundary: Not claim approval guidance, legal sufficiency proof, liability determination, or coverage determination.

### Appendix E - Coverage Boundary Question Map

Purpose: Preserve Chapter 21's coverage-boundary question structure.

Boundary: Not coverage opinion, policy interpretation, legal advice, or claim-handling instruction.

### Appendix F - Source and Claim Boundary Notes

Purpose: Consolidate source caveats, author synthesis labels, and claim-boundary caveats.

Boundary: Source support and safer language only; not legal, insurance, or underwriting advice.

### Appendix G - Final Non-Claim Language Register

Purpose: Preserve final restricted-claim and safer-language rules from Chapter 27.

Boundary: QA and publication guardrail; not a legal disclaimer drafted as legal advice.

### Appendix H - Table Inventory

Purpose: Provide a full list of accepted tables T-02-01 through T-27-01.

Boundary: Inventory only; not artifact layout authorization.

### Appendix I - Assembly Change Log

Purpose: Track R3H full-body assembly edits, repetition compression, note consolidation, and non-substantive continuity changes.

Boundary: Internal change log only; not a publication or release record.

## Appendix Drafting Rules for Later Waves

1. Do not draft appendix content in R3G.
2. Do not create appendix tables that duplicate full body tables without a clear reason.
3. Do not turn appendix templates into standards or checklists.
4. Preserve source caveats and non-claim language.
5. Keep appendices subordinate to the body, not a replacement for the body argument.
6. Do not create public artifacts from appendix skeletons.
